FAQ

Quick answers before you order.

How do I receive my clips?

We send them after we receive your VOD link and payment.

What games do you clip?

Any game. We started with Apex Legends and Dota 2, and we also clip other shooters, MOBAs, variety and Just Chatting streams.

How do I send my VOD?

Email a link to your stream to nexclips.feminine462@aleeas.com: a Twitch or Kick VOD, a YouTube video (unlisted is fine), or a Google Drive or Dropbox file. Add timestamps if there are moments you want us to use.

What do I get back?

Vertical 9:16 MP4 clips with captions, ready for TikTok, Reels and Shorts, plus a thumbnail image for each clip.

How does the free launch offer work?

Early clients get their first 5 clips free. In return, we ask for a short, honest testimonial we can share.

What's a Stream Bot Setup?

A one-time, done-for-you setup of AI helpers for your stream: a go-live checklist bot, auto-clipping of your streams, and a social post maker. We set it up for you, and it's yours to keep.

Can I cancel the Weekly Plan?

Yes. The plan is month to month and you can cancel anytime.
